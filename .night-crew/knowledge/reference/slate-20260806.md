### 🛑 The global Playwright lock

**Dispatch is CONCURRENT across three tracks (operator's choice at sign-off, 2026-08-05), and this
rule is the condition that choice was made under.** Six of the seven cards want the full suite, and
`sync.spec.js` is load-sensitive. **No two Playwright suites may ever be in flight at once, in any
track, for any reason** — nor may a Go suite or an RLS suite run alongside one.

The orchestrator owns the lock; subagents do not coordinate with each other. A card that reaches its
Playwright gate while another track holds the lock **waits**. A suite run that turns out to have
overlapped another is **discarded and re-run**, not reasoned about — run `20260802` produced a
51.7m / 7-failure gate this way and it had to be thrown out. Serialize suite runs in this order when
several are queued: **C1 first** (its estimate is the widest and its value is independent), then
Track A, then Track B.

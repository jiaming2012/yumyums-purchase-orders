### Card B1 · `sync-rxdb-collections-and-table-contract` — PLANNED, SLATE-READY, foundation

Define the RxDB collections for **checklists, templates, responses, approvals** mirroring the
current Postgres domain model, each satisfying the self-hosted table contract the roadmap records.

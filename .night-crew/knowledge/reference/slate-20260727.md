### Card C — `sync-rxdb-schema-and-replication`

**Footprint:** `workflows.html` + a new client-side sync layer.

The centre of gravity for Activity 1, and the riskiest card in the slate. Scope was reduced
2026-07-26 by the fan-out (browser delivery/storage/SW → `sync-rxdb-browser-delivery-spike`, the
user-visible notice → `sync-rxdb-conflict-notice-ui`). What remains: the collections, the
per-table SQL contract, the `replicateSupabase` wiring, the **`_modified` semantics call**, and
the **headless, testable `conflictHandler`**. It also inherits the client-construction helper and
the `@supabase/supabase-js` pin + upgrade smoke test from `sync-jwt-bridge-endpoint` — this is
where the client is actually constructed.

**The conflict policy is this card's real work** (T-22 decision 50). RxDB's default is
unconditional master-wins; no clock participates and a strictly-later local write is discarded
silently — reproduced four times, most recently at triage. The decided policy is a **field-level
three-way merge**: different fields edited by different people all survive; only a genuine
same-field clash falls back to master-wins, and then `conflict$` must surface it with the
discarded value recoverable. Tractable because `assumedMasterState` is in `RxConflictHandlerInput`
(`conflict-handling.d.ts:10`); it is **optional** in the type, so the rule needs a defined
fallback when absent.

**Five obligations added at morning triage 2026-07-26 (T-23), all binding:**

1. **Row visibility — decision 55.** PORT `ResolveEntityAccess`
   (`backend/internal/sync/ops.go:474`); do not invent a predicate. Project
   `template_assignments ⋈ users` into the sync DB the way `hq_grant_projection` projects grants,
   and express RLS as an `EXISTS` against it. Two inherited properties are **knowing, not
   accidental**: the resolver never filters on `assignment_role`, and the
   `roles && ARRAY['admin','superadmin']` arm is unconditional. **Changing either is a SEPARATE
   card** — do not vary substrate and permission semantics in one night.
2. **Origin shape — decision 62.** DECLARE same-origin-fronted vs cross-origin as the card's
   **FIRST spec line**, and **cost the reverse proxy** if same-origin.
3. **Umbrella slug expansion — decision 56, operator-confirmed 2026-07-26.** Expand umbrella slugs
   to constituent per-tab grants at mint so `hq_grants` matches reachable surface.
4. **Cache-key design — decision 57's deferred half.** Card A clears `api-cache` on logout; C
   decides whether it is keyed by identity.
5. **Re-add the vendored bundle to the precache** on adoption — the counterpart to Card A change 3.

**🛑 PARK TRIGGERS, explicit:**
- The `_modified` semantics call turns out to need an operator answer rather than a defensible

- The origin-shape declaration (obligation 2) cannot be made without an operator decision on

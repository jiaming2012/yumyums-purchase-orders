## Why the order is what it is

The naive order is "F5 first" — it closes live exposure and it is the card that got dropped last
run. **Footprint analysis overrides that**, for one concrete reason: the signed design's
**mixed-grant case (§1.6, a Trends-only user)** is untestable while Trends does not exist. F5
gating a surface that F1/F3 have not yet built would ship a gate with a hole in its own test
matrix, then need a second pass. With no budget pressure, drop-risk protection is worth nothing
and seam cleanliness is worth everything — so F5 gates a **complete** surface in **one** pass.

Exposure cost of this choice: `/inventory/cost` and `#s6` stay logged-in-only for ~4h longer
within the same night. They close tonight either way.

  07-21's A1 leg was orchestration stall, not work.

  `cp`. A subagent breached this on 07-22, self-disclosed, and recovered.
